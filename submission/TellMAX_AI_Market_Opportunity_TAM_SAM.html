1. Strategic Problem & Headline Hook

Cisco Partners and enterprise IT teams are drowning in alert fatigue. Global 2000 enterprises lose $600B annually to unplanned downtime (Source: Splunk & Cisco 2025/2026 State of Digital Resilience Report). Critical infrastructure environments generate thousands of daily alerts across fragmented IT and OT systems, stretching MTTR from 45 to 90 minutes. TellMAX.AI solves this by acting as the digital clone of the network engineer — always on site, always available. MAX bridges the human expertise gap, eliminating tool sprawl by unifying Splunk, Meraki, Cisco Spaces, Webex, and Catalyst into an active execution engine.

2. Three Core Breakthroughs

  • 1. The Brain of Splunk: Acts as Splunk's "frontal lobe" — translating raw SPL telemetry into human dialogue, cross-referencing IT/OT context with technical PDFs via RAG, and making autonomous decisions in <5 seconds.
  • 2. Partner Superpowers & MTTR Reduction: Cuts L0/L1 incident resolution time by up to 85% (Source: TellMAX.AI Production Benchmark). Hands-free voice (via Cisco CUBE + Webex Calling) enables engineers to execute cable tests and port bounces on the move.
  • 3. Sovereign Architecture (UCS AI Pods): Migration path to run 100% on-premise on Cisco UCS AI Pods — zero data leakage for regulated enterprises.
